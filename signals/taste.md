# Owner Taste Memory

Permanent, hand-curated. Unlike the rolling ratings/lessons windows, nothing
here expires — these are all-time truths, not recent trends. Edit by hand;
the pipeline never writes to this file.

## Gold standards — the execution bar

- **2026-04-28, drenched terracotta Specimen (risk 8).** A single hue
  committed across ~70% of the viewport, type waterfall reversed out of the
  field, labels and size markers treated as design material, not
  afterthoughts. Rated the benchmark: a committed gesture, not a hedge. This
  is the bar every build is measured against.
- **2026-06-13, fairway Split.** Left panel is the thesis (one enormous
  word, drenched fairway green), right panel is the evidence (clean
  cataloged rows). The split works because the two halves need each other —
  productive irony, the word for smallness rendered as the biggest object on
  the page.

- **2026-09-01, trophy-gold scoreboard flood.** The homepage only. Gold
  ground, near-black condensed caps at poster scale, one ochre line for the
  third stroke, and no decorative furniture anywhere — the scoreboard conceit
  carried entirely by type and colour. The inner pages did not hold the same
  bar, but this hero is the gesture to keep reaching for.

## Standing complaints — do not repeat

- **Header keeps breaking.** Called out three ratings running (2026-07-20,
  -22, -23): "header is messed up," rounded corners read wrong, top
  navigation disliked outright. Header is not a solved problem — treat it
  as first-class design surface, not a template default.
- **Reads as templated, not designed.** 2026-07-22: "still feels like it is
  cycling through 4 or 5 different templates." Variance between builds must
  come from a genuine compositional decision, never from swapping a layout
  off a shortlist.
- **Brand lockup must be the real circular mark, never a placeholder.**
  2026-07-10: a shipped build kept the wordmark text but dropped the mark —
  graded down. 2026-07-22: "missing my personal brand, it is a gray box."
  The circular mark is the fixed element of the brand contract; wherever
  the mockup shows it (nav, header, footer), the render must too.

- **Display and body must share a skeleton.** 2026-09-01 paired an
  ultra-condensed poster grotesque with a soft, wide humanist sans that shared
  no width axis, terminal treatment or vertical proportion with it. The
  homepage survived it on eleven words; the case study read as a poster
  headline pasted onto a default web page. Pick a body face that answers the
  display face's construction, or make the opposition explicit and set the body
  large enough that it looks chosen. A body face picked for neutrality is the
  same as no pairing at all.

- **Spend the middle of the type scale.** 2026-09-01 inner pages jumped from a
  ~150px title straight to ~16px body with nothing between 24px and 100px,
  which is why they read as "big title, then default text". Every inner page
  needs at least one mid-register element: a standfirst under the title, a
  section head, or a pull figure. A page with only two type sizes is a page
  where the layout is doing no work.

- **Cap the measure with a token, not with whatever the grid hands you.**
  2026-09-01 body ran ~80 characters per line on the case study and ~95 on the
  work index, at 1.63 and 2.05 leading for the same paragraph of copy. Set body
  columns to 60-70 characters and hold leading at 1.5-1.6 for that measure on
  every route. Loose leading does not rescue a long line, it separates it
  further.

- **Small labels bind downward, and they have to survive their own ground.**
  2026-09-01 set PROBLEM / APPROACH / OUTCOME 14px above their paragraph and
  27px below the block before them, so each label headed nothing and the page
  read as one grey column with three orange specks in it. Space above a label
  is at least twice the space below it. Letterspaced accent caps are also the
  first thing to fail contrast and the last thing anyone checks — the homepage
  eyebrow measured roughly 3.4:1 ochre-on-gold. Check every accent-on-ground
  and muted-on-ground pair the palette can produce.

- **One left rail per page.** 2026-09-01 hung the case-study eyebrow at x=123,
  its title at x=130 and its body at x=95 — one page, two competing margins
  35px apart, and visible. Everything on a page hangs off a single rail;
  optical adjustment is reserved for punctuation and round letterforms.

- **An empty column has to be a decision.** 2026-09-01 left ~690px of flat
  ground beside a left-aligned text column under a full-bleed hero, while the
  page's strongest evidence — a ten-client list including Zeldman, Rolex and
  The Nature Conservancy — sat buried mid-sentence in running prose. Either
  fill the second column with what belongs there (role, year, client set,
  figure) or narrow and inset the block so the space reads as margin. A wide
  void beside a narrow column reads as a layout that failed, not as restraint.

- **Nav must clear the flood.** 2026-09-01 rendered the nav as two orphan
  underline rules on `/` and `/work/spaceman` — the label colour collided with
  the gold ground and only the borders survived. Whatever the day's flood
  colour is, the nav labels are checked against it, not against the default
  surface. Extends the header complaint above.

## Claude's Rationale

The visual specification has been thoroughly designed and critic-approved. My role is to translate the director's concrete choices into a working PandaCSS preset that enforces the established system. The specification mandates warm cream neutrals (H:65°, 6–8% saturation across nine steps), spring green as the single dominant accent (H:128°), and slate teal as secondary structure (H:200°). The spacing scale (4–96px) enforces the generous, deliberately paced Split archetype layout; line heights (1.05–1.75) and letter-spacings (-0.03em to +0.14em) create the typographic rhythm that respects 13.8 hours of daylight. Zero shadows, high contrast (17:1 on headings), and warm undertones throughout create a page illuminated by 88% gibbous moon—everything visible, nothing dramatic. The preset bridges the director's layout vision and the typography chassis, ensuring components build on a consistent foundation.

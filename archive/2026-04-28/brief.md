## Claude's Rationale

This design splits vertically into two temperature worlds: warm oat-milk LEFT (housing a Millman quote in meditative contemplation) and cool slate-pewter RIGHT (holding a tight editorial feed). Cobalt 245° serves as the single accent, reserved exclusively for the RIGHT half's ticker glyph and measles underline — it never crosses the seam. The warm neutral scale (H:55°) tints toward the LEFT's biological light, while the cool scale becomes pure operation on the RIGHT. Spacing honors the Split archetype: LEFT breathes (80px top, 48px+ gaps around the quote), RIGHT compresses (0.5px hairlines between rows, 3vw padding). Zero shadow, zero radius — the temperature contrast and the seam itself carry all visual weight. This is print discipline: flat, committed, no softening.

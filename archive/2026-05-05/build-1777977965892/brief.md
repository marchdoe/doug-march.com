## Claude's Rationale

This design system honors the brief's explicit deviation from the mandate's teal zone (190°–215°) in favor of warm olive-green at 90°, which maps directly to "spring light filtered through aged glass" rather than technical cool. The mandate conflict is brief-driven and justified: teal would betray the "productive and slightly deflated" mood that demands warmth, not restraint. The Split archetype drives spacing strategy—fixed left panel (340px) uses tight internal density (8–12px gaps between signal elements, 24px between blocks) with generous padding (56px vertical, 40px horizontal), anchoring identity and ambient data. The scrollable right column breathes at 64px section padding and 48px gaps, honoring the brief's demand for spaciousness and the 13.8 hours of May daylight. A single spring-green accent carries full visual authority; the secondary terracotta accent appears only once, framing the Ming-Dao Deng quote with a soft Mother's Day warmth moment. Support tokens—line heights (1.00–1.75), letter spacings (tight to widest)—pair with the Space Grotesk / Work Sans chassis to reinforce "readable, not soft" voice: geometric precision in labels and polyglot tags, generous breathing (1.55) in body text. The waning moon signal sets contrast calibration: body text at 4.5:1 against the light background feels present but not harsh, like afternoon light through aged paper.

## Claude's Rationale

The brief from Doug's director is remarkably specific and architectural — it's not a mood to interpret but a visual specification to faithfully execute through tokens. The system I've built prioritizes two core tensions that define May 3rd: **lunar drama and spring clarity**.

The color palette anchors on a single cool spring mint (H:160°, S:65%, positioned at the edge of the mandate's open window) that carries interactive weight without aggression. A warm amber secondary accent (H:35°, complementary contrast) appears sparingly in emotional signals only—Tigers score, Dickens quote border, moon percentage. The neutral scale deliberately desaturates to 5-8% saturation, preserving the clean, directional quality of 13.7-hour May daylight: bright without glare, cool without coldness. The waning gibbous moon (94.5%) translates into tonal architecture: near-black Awwwards room cards erupt against pale panels, creating sharp chiaroscuro that fulfills the brief's demand for "dramatic contrast."

Spacing tokens respect the Split archetype's dual nature: generous gaps (32–64px) between signal blocks permit leisurely Sunday exploration, while micro-rhythms (4–8px) within components signal precision and craft. Typography tokens (lineHeights 1.0–1.82, letterSpacings -0.03em to +0.14em) manifest the "personality in spacing" the brief requires — Radiohead's angular geometry in tight display spacing, GBV's scrappy warmth in loose small-cap widths, MMJ's introspection in the Dickens quote's generous line-height.

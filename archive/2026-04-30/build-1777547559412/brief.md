## Claude's Rationale

The hero phrase chose itself. Signals conspired: tonight is Walpurgis Night (April 30, the witches' eve), the moon is at 99.3% illumination, and Hacker News is debating the Zig project's decision to reject AI contributions — a technical community standing up to its own tools, its own friends. The Dumbledore quote from `signals.quote` named this precisely: "It takes just as much bravery to stand up to our friends." Condensed to drop the enemies clause, the remaining fragment is harder, stranger, more resonant — and Dumbledore is a wizard, so the Walpurgis alignment is unforced. The phrase earns marquee scale today.

The phrase is a declaration, not a description. Declarations want the Specimen archetype — type IS the design, nothing competes, nothing decorates. The Specimen hasn't appeared in 10 days and genuinely serves this brief better than any other archetype. Big Shoulders Display (the display half of `big-shoulders-atkinson`, ratio 1.618) was built for signage — proclamation boards, wayfinding, protest signs — which is exactly the energy of a moral statement at poster scale. Atkinson Hyperlegible handles attribution and signal data with clinical legibility, contrasting the drama without competing.

The palette flows from darkness, not from palette theory. Walpurgis Night is violet. The full moon (99.3%) gives us permission to use near-white type against the deepest possible surface — `#0D0A1E`, below even neutral.900 on the violet-tinted scale — achieving ~19:1 contrast for the hero text. Gold (62°, permitted zone) plays the role of fire: it appears on the moon indicator, the Walpurgis word in the context strip, the sports win arrow, and interactive hover states. It never crowds. The phrase gets the darkness it needs to glow.
